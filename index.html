Questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

Pricing is set for each card and depends on the area and what is going on that season. Text me and I will send current pricing for your area along with your free mockup.

What is the free mockup?

I design your ad for free, using your logo, your offer and your info, so you can see exactly how it will look on the card. You can ask for changes, and you only move forward if you like it.

Is my category open?

Each card has one business per category. Text me your business and category, and I will check it for the card you want.

What sizes are the ads?

Coupon and single ads are 3.5 x 1.8 inches, doubles take two spots, and quadruples take four. Sizes can vary slightly by card, so I confirm them when we talk.

When does each card mail?

Cards mail on a set date for each area, usually near the start of a season. Each area page shows the current date and ad deadline.

Can I be on more than one card?

Yes. Many businesses want to reach more than one area. Text me and we will plan it together.
