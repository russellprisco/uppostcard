What sizes are there?

Coupon and single ads are 3.5" x 1.8" (one spot). A double takes two spots (7.2" x 1.8" wide or 3.5" x 3.8" tall). A quadruple takes four spots (7.1" x 3.7").

Is it exactly 15,000 homes?

It is close to 15,000, but not an exact number. The card is mailed by U.S. Postal Service carrier routes, and the number of homes on each route changes slightly. That means the total varies a little from card to card. Every home on the routes we choose gets one, so we say roughly 15,000.

What would it cost to mail this myself?

Postage alone is 26 cents per household, so mailing 15,000 homes on your own is $3,900 before printing or design. Add those and most businesses land between $6,000 and $10,000 or more.
